Algorithms
==========

Every estimator SCPP provides, generated from ``soft_clustering/_registry.py``. ⚡ marks estimators that need the ``deep`` extra. The **Output** codes say what ``memberships_`` holds:

- **F** — fuzzy membership (rows sum to one)
- **T** — possibilistic typicality (rows need not sum to one)
- **P** — posterior probability (rows sum to one)
- **E** — pignistic probability of a credal partition (rows sum to one)
- **R** — rough-set membership read off lower/upper approximations
- **A** — non-negative affiliation strength (unnormalised)
- **M** — mixed-membership or topic proportion (rows sum to one)
- **S** — soft assignment from a relaxation (rows sum to one)
- **D** — binary degree of match; a sample may match several clusters or none
- **H** — crisp consensus partition, one-hot

The **Status** levels say how far each implementation has been verified:

- **validated** — agrees with an independent implementation, or reduces exactly to one in a limit, in tests/test_external_agreement.py.
- **reference** — implements the update equations of the cited publication, checked against it in the 0.2.0 audit; invariants and planted-structure recovery are tested, but no independent implementation was compared.
- **adapted** — implements the cited method with documented deviations; see the Notes or 'Deviation' paragraphs of the estimator's docstring.
- **unverified** — follows the cited method as implemented by contributors; the equations have not been checked against the publication. Recovery of planted structure is tested, nothing more.
